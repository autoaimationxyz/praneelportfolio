A connected
campaign approach.

The objective was to generate property enquiries across discovery, search intent and re-engagement.

The campaign combined Meta prospecting, Google Search, Meta remarketing and Google remarketing over 90 days. The reported spend was ₹14,88,989 against an approved ₹15,00,000 budget.

Client work delivered at ZycomTech (Zycom Technologies & Services). Client identity is withheld.
