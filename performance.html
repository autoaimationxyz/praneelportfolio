The brief

Bring in property enquiries across three moments: people discovering the project, people actively searching for homes, and people who had already shown interest. The approved budget was ₹15,00,000 for 90 days.

The approach

Each channel got an estimated range for leads, cost per lead and CTR before launch. Leads were tracked weekly by channel in Excel, and budget moved toward whatever converted. Results went back to the client in regular performance reviews with next-step recommendations.

Learn. Ramp up. Peak. Optimise.

  1. 01, weeks 1–2Learning

    Let the platforms learn, test audiences and set the baseline.

  2. 02, weeks 3–4Ramp-up

    Shift budget to what converted; lead volume climbs fast.

  3. 03, weeks 5–8Peak

    Highest volume across every channel, topping out at 89 in week 7.

  4. 04, weeks 9–13Optimise

    A steady, efficient run that kept results consistent to the end.
