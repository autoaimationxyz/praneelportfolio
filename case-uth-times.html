U’th Times, social content, Apr–Sep 2025

25K to 110K in six months.

The brief

U’th Times wanted a social channel that young audiences would actually follow: fast, funny, relatable video that sounded like the brand.

What I did

  • Planned content ideas with the team and wrote them into formats that suit Reels and Shorts.
  • Filmed and directed shoots, then edited each video from rough cut to final.
  • Kept every edit on-brand, with pacing built for the first three seconds and the rewatch.

The result

The channel grew from 25,000 to 110,000 followers over six months. That’s a team result I was part of, not mine alone, and only the start and end figures were recorded.
