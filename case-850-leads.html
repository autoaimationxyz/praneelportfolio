ZycomTech, real estate, Meta & Google Ads

850 leads in 90 days.

A real-estate client needed enquiries. I ran their Meta and Google campaigns for 13 weeks on a simple plan: learn fast, scale what works, then hold the line.

The brief

The client wanted a steady flow of property enquiries, and needed to see them coming in every week, not just a total at the end. The agency set a ₹15 lakh budget across 90 days.

My role

I was the client’s point of contact at ZycomTech and ran the campaign day to day.

  • Set up Meta prospecting, Google Search, and remarketing on both platforms.
  • Tracked leads every week by channel in Excel.
  • Ran performance reviews with the client and recommended where to move budget.
  • Coordinated design and web teams for creatives and landing pages.

The plan

Weeks 1–2Learn

Let the platforms find the audience; watch cost per lead closely.

Weeks 3–4Ramp up

Push budget into the ad sets and keywords that were converting.

Weeks 5–8Peak

Maximum volume across every channel; 89 leads in week 7.

Weeks 9–13Optimise

Hold a stable, efficient run and finish under budget.

Meta prospecting brought the most volume. Google Search cost more per lead but had the highest click-through rate, meaning people were actively searching. Remarketing on both platforms brought people back at the lowest cost per lead.

What it shows

16 lakh impressions, 37,120 clicks and 850 leads, delivered ₹11,011 under budget. These are reported leads from the campaign report, not closed sales; revenue figures weren’t shared with me. The client’s name is kept private.
