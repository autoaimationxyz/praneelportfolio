TAKE1, short-form video

A million views in three months.

TAKE1 needed social video people would watch to the end. I helped plan, produce and edit it, with every cut made for retention.

Views
1,000,000+
Timeframe
Three months
My part
Planning, production, editing
Focus

The idea

Short-form platforms reward one thing above all: people who stay. So the work started with the hook and worked backwards, with every second earning the next.

What I did

  • Planned video ideas and the structure for each one.
  • Helped set up the production workflow so videos shipped on a steady schedule.
  • Edited for storytelling, pacing and retention: tight hooks, no dead air, clear payoffs.

The result

The content passed one million cumulative views in three months. Views count plays, not unique people, and this was a team effort I contributed to.
