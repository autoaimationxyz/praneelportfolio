01 / THE CONTEXT

A real-estate client needed enquiries across discovery, search intent and re-engagement over a 90-day campaign.

02 / THE ROLE

At ZycomTech, Praneel ran the campaign end to end, combining Meta prospecting, Google Search and remarketing, tracking leads weekly by channel and recommending optimisations to the client.
